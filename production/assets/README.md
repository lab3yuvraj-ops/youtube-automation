# Local channel assets

Put the private channel intro at `production/assets/channel-intro.mp4`.
It is intentionally ignored by Git, but the final editor requires it and uses
only its first five seconds before cross-fading into every story.

Put the transparent channel logo at `production/assets/channel-logo.png`.
It is applied on every final export at the reference position: lower-right,
inset from the right and bottom edges rather than flush in the corner. At
1280x720, its rendered height is 76px with 72px right and 75px bottom margins.

The logo is ordinary channel branding. Do not use it to conceal, remove, or
obscure third-party watermarks.
