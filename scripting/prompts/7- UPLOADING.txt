THUMBNAIL STYLE REFERENCE GUIDE
Derived from analysis of 16 top-performing Hindi horror thumbnails. These principles apply directly to your thumbnail prompts in Step 4, Part 5.

Element | What Works In This Niche
Dominant Style | 2D semi-realistic cartoon — bold outlines, smooth shading, dramatic rim light. High detail on the central figure; cinematic poster feel.
Colour Energy | Teal-blue moonlit night base is default. Fiery orange-red for demonic/hell themes. Purple-magenta glow for cursed objects & magic. High saturation, dark vignette.

10 THUMBNAIL LAYOUTS — Rotate Through All 10, Never Repeat
● Layout 1 — VICTIM + ENTITY BEHIND: Terrified human in the foreground (60–65% of image height), supernatural entity looming behind. Title placed top-right or bottom. The single most-used niche layout.
● Layout 2 — SINGLE ENTITY CLOSE-UP: One menacing chudail/pret/demon face filling 70–80% of the frame, glowing eyes, atmospheric background barely visible. Title at the bottom or right.
● Layout 3 — GHOST GRABS VICTIM: Entity clutching/embracing a screaming victim from behind, both faces visible. Forest or indoor night background. Title top-right.
● Layout 4 — TANTRIK RITUAL TRIO: Tantrik/witch in the centre with magic strings or fire, two terrified victims flanking them, glowing magic circle + diya. Title bottom-centre.
● Layout 5 — SCREAMING VICTIM MID-ACTION: Gory/possessed victim filling the frame, blood scratches, subway/forest/room setting. Brushstroke title top-left.
● Layout 6 — COFFIN / TABOOT PROCESSION: Hooded red demons carrying a glowing coffin with a fleeing victim at the side, graveyard setting. Title top-right. (Best for serials.)
● Layout 7 — EMOTIONAL / CURSED LOVE SPLIT: Human on one side and spirit on the other reaching toward each other, warm vs cool color split, multi-line Hindi tagline.
● Layout 8 — GROUP DREAD: Multiple repeated faceless/veiled figures (e.g., banjaran), candle/lantern lighting, title on the left in Devanagari.
● Layout 9 — SKULL + ENTITY + GRAVE: Fiery orange color palette, tombstone with carved name/dates, hellish glow, chudail rising behind, large Devanagari title.
● Layout 10 — LANTERN ENVIRONMENT + LONE FIGURE: Single ghost or victim holding a lantern in a well, graveyard, or forest with heavy fog. Transliteration title at the bottom.

TEXT STYLES — Assign One Per Prompt, Never Repeat
● Style A — RED BRUSH + WHITE SUBTITLE: First word in red horror brushstroke ALL CAPS, "HORROR STORY" in white below. (Default niche style.)
● Style B — DEVANAGARI RED BRUSH: Hindi title in dripping red brush font with a small white tagline below.
● Style C — BIG BOTTOM TITLE: Large transliterated title spanning the lower third in red brush lettering.
● Style D — NUMBERED SEQUEL: Title with a large red or white sequel number beside it (e.g., 2, 3).
● Style E — WHITE CAPS + RED ACCENT: White all-caps headline with 1–2 important words highlighted in red.
● Style F — MULTI-LINE TAGLINE: Main title plus 2–3 lines of emotional Hindi tagline (ideal for cursed-love themes).
● Style G — GOLD / CARVED ON STONE: Character name carved into a tombstone with the Devanagari title in red.
● Style H — GLOW CAPS: Title in glowing red/orange capital letters matching fire or magic-circle lighting.

MANDATORY IN EVERY THUMBNAIL PROMPT
● Central figure fills 60–80% of frame in portrait compositions.
● Expressions: Victims: terror. Entities: menace + glowing eyes. Calm expressions only as dramatic irony.
● 2D semi-realistic cartoon style — bold outlines, smooth shading, NOT photorealistic.
● Dark edge vignette + high-contrast cinematic look; no watermark in the generated image.
